1. Executive Summary
ZeroTask will be developed in phases over approximately 12-14 weeks, following a milestone-based approach. Each phase focuses on a specific module, allowing regular reviews, testing, and feedback before moving to the next stage. This approach reduces development risks while ensuring the application remains scalable, secure, and aligned with business requirements.

2. Project Objectives
•	Build a centralized work execution platform.
•	Replace scattered communication channels.
•	Improve task visibility and accountability.
•	Simplify approvals and collaboration.
•	Provide real-time project tracking.
•	Deliver a stable Android APK for internal use.
•	Build a scalable foundation for future enhancements.
